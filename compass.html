    How to play

    1. Guess any country. You have eight tries to find the secret one.
    2. Distance and direction show how far the secret country is from your guess (as the crow flies) and which way to head on a map.
    3. Region turns green for the same subregion (e.g. Western Europe), yellow for the same continent, red otherwise.
    4. Population turns green within 10%. Otherwise ⬆ means the secret country has more people, ⬇ fewer.

    Every UN member and observer state can be the answer: 195 countries. A new daily country unlocks at your midnight.
