How to play

  1. Look at the known photo and its year.
  2. Decide whether the next photo was taken earlier (older) or later (newer).
  3. Keep going. Each right answer adds one to your streak; the first wrong one ends the run.

Two photos in a row are always at least three years apart. The daily run has 49 calls to make, the same for everyone.
