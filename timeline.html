    How to play

    1. Order the five photographs from oldest to newest. Drag them, or use the arrow buttons.
    2. Check your order. Photos in the right place lock in and show their year.
    3. Fix the rest. You have three tries.

    A photo locked on the first try scores 200, on the second 100, on the third 50. A perfect timeline is 1,000. No two photos are within five years of each other.
